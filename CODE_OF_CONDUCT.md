# Code of conduct

The Fisher Index welcomes evidence-based disagreement and does not tolerate harassment, demeaning language, or attacks on contributors.

Be precise about claims and generous toward people. Critique sources, arguments, code, and wording—not a contributor’s identity or presumed motives. Historical discussion may involve racism, eugenics, ableism, sexism, and other harms; discuss these directly without reproducing abuse gratuitously.

Project maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and participation that undermine a safe, rigorous collaborative environment. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub profile contact information.
