## Why it matters

A time series may contain a strong repeating cycle even when its phase and frequency are unknown. Fisher’s test asks whether one Fourier frequency captures too large a share of the series’ total variation to be plausible under white noise.

## Intuition

Under noise alone, spectral power is spread irregularly but not usually dominated by one frequency. A sinusoidal signal creates a pronounced periodogram peak. Dividing the largest peak by all spectral power makes the statistic independent of the unknown noise variance.

## Prerequisites

For an evenly spaced, mean-adjusted series, compute periodogram ordinates $I_1,\ldots,I_m$ at the Fourier frequencies. The classical null assumes independent Gaussian white noise.

## Formal statement

Fisher’s statistic is

$$g=\frac{\max_j I_j}{\sum_{j=1}^{m} I_j}.$$

For suitable $x$, its exact upper-tail probability can be written by inclusion–exclusion as

$$P(g\ge x)=\sum_{k=1}^{\lfloor1/x\rfloor}(-1)^{k-1}{m\choose k}(1-kx)^{m-1}.$$

## Worked example

If five ordinates are $[1,2,2,3,12]$, then $g=12/20=0.6$. The statistic records concentration, not raw amplitude: multiplying every ordinate by ten leaves $g$ unchanged.

## History and attribution

Fisher presented the test in his 1929 paper on harmonic analysis. Later spectral theory clarified its assumptions and extended testing beyond Fourier-grid sinusoids. This test should not be called simply “Fisher’s G-test” without context: the log-likelihood-ratio G statistic for categorical data is a different method.

## Modern use

The test remains a reference method for periodicity and spectral peaks. Related maximum-periodogram tests appear in signal processing, astronomy, rhythm analysis, and genomics, often with simulation or multiple-frequency corrections.

## Limits and debates

Autocorrelation, trends, uneven sampling, non-Gaussian noise, and frequencies between Fourier bins change the null distribution. Selecting preprocessing or a frequency range after viewing the spectrum also changes the test. A large peak need not represent a stable physical cycle.
